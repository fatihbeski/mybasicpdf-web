FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Where are my documents stored?

All your documents are stored locally on your physical device. Mybasic PDF App does not use cloud servers, ensuring 100% privacy and data sovereignty.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple. You can cancel or change your plan in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Can I recover a deleted file?

No. Since your files are only stored on your device and never uploaded to our servers, once a file is deleted from the app, it is permanently removed.

How to get the best scan quality?

For best results, place your document on a flat surface with high contrast background and ensure you have good, natural lighting.
