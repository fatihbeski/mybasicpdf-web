Mybasic PDF App
Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment ("Local-First")

Mybasic PDF App is engineered on a strict local-first architecture. All document processing, scanning, merging, splitting, and conversions occur entirely on your physical device. Your files, documents, and images are never uploaded to external servers, and we never have access to your data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mybasic PDF App ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent, secure environment for mobile document workflows. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices when you use our mobile application.

By installing and using Mybasic PDF App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the application.

2. Document Security & Local-First Processing

Unlike traditional cloud-based document utilities, Mybasic PDF App prioritizes absolute data sovereignty.

•
Zero Server Uploads: When you use the app to scan files using the camera, convert images, merge multiple files, or split paths, the computational workflow is executed entirely within your device's native hardware limits.
•
No Cloud Infrastructure: We do not operate cloud servers to store, cache, or look at your files. Your documents remain under your complete physical and digital custody.

3. Information Collection and Use

We practice strict data minimization. The app is built to be fully functional without collecting your personal information.

3.1 Personal Identification Data

Mybasic PDF App does not require user registration, profiles, or account creation. We explicitly do not collect, store, or transmit:

• Names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

• Mailing addresses or contact lists.

3.2 Payment and Subscription Data

The app offers Premium subscriptions handled entirely through Apple App Store Commerce. All transaction details, billing info, and credit card processing are securely managed directly by the App Store. We never see or store your financial payment tokens or credit card information.

3.3 Performance Analytics and Crash Reporting

To maintain app stability and optimize performance, we integrate minimal, third-party diagnostic SDKs (such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry). These services collect strictly anonymous, aggregated technical metrics, including:

• Device hardware model and operating system version.

• App crash logs and performance bottlenecks.

• Timestamps of app anomalies.

4. Device Permissions Required

To provide core document management functionalities, the app requires specific system permissions. You can adjust or revoke these permissions at any time via your iOS settings.

•
Camera Permission: Used exclusively to capture physical documents using your device's camera module for our scanning engine. Captured images are strictly processed on-device and never shared.
•
Storage / Files Permission: Used solely to let you import existing images or documents from your file systems, and to save newly generated PDFs back to your local drive.

5. Regulatory Compliance & Global Standards

5.1 GDPR Compliance

Because Mybasic PDF App does not process, store, or transmit personal data to our servers, we do not operate as a Data Controller or Data Processor under GDPR definitions. For anonymous diagnostics, our legitimate interest is app stability.

5.2 CCPA Compliance

We do not "sell" or "share" your personal information. No user tracking profiles are built or traded.

5.3 Apple App Store Privacy Manifest

In line with Apple's strict privacy rules, we certify that no data collected is linked to the user's identity, and diagnostic data is used purely for application optimization.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. Because we design our platform to bypass any collection of personally identifiable information, we do not knowingly track or store any information from children.
